HelBot v@VERSION@

A MeshCore chat bot that runs on the radio itself: ping, path, wx, sun and more, with no computer or server. For the Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4 only.

This browser can't flash over USB. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer, or the esptool steps below. Flashing needs this page to be opened over HTTPS.

Plug the board in with a USB data cable first. Installing replaces the firmware on the board.

1. Install

  1. Press Install above and pick the board's serial port.
  2. If the port isn't listed, or flashing won't start: hold BOOT, tap RST, release BOOT, then try again.
  3. Wait for it to finish (about a minute). The board restarts by itself.
Keeping the old firmware? Back it up before installing: 
esptool.py --chip esp32s3 --port <port> read_flash 0 0x1000000 backup.bin
Re-installing a newer version over a bot keeps its name, identity and settings.

2. Connect it to your WiFi

  1. Power the board from a charger or power bank. Some computers hold it in the bootloader.
  2. With no WiFi saved, it opens a network called MeshBot-XXXXXX. The password is shown on the board's screen. Join it from your phone and the setup page opens by itself.
  3. Choose your WiFi network and set a web password for the bot. The board restarts, joins your WiFi and shows its address on the screen.

3. Set it up

  1. Open the board's address in a browser and log in with user admin and your web password.
  2. On Settings: choose the bot's name, your location and UTC offset (for sun, moon and wx), the channels it answers on, and which commands are on. Press save, then restart.
  3. In the MeshCore app, connect to the board like any companion radio, set your region's radio settings, and join the channel the bot should answer on.
  4. Send ping on that channel. The bot answers pong.

MQTT observer (optional, off by default)

The bot can publish the packets it hears to a MeshCore analyzer. It stays off until you pick a broker and enter your IATA location code (a nearby airport code) on the Settings page, then restart. The community brokers are shared, volunteer-run infrastructure: use your own region's code and follow the operators' rules.

Updating later

No cable needed. Download firmware.bin below, open http://<board address>/update, log in and upload it. The board restarts into the new version; the version is shown on the status page.

Troubleshooting

The port isn't listed, or it stays on the bootloader Use a data cable (not charge-only), try another USB port, and use BOOT + RST as in step 1. After flashing, power the board from a charger instead of the computer.
I forgot the WiFi or want to switch networks Hold the BOOT button for 10 seconds. That wipes the saved WiFi (the web password stays) and the board opens its setup network again.
I can't reach the settings page Use the address shown on the board's screen. Your computer must be on the same WiFi. If the board can't join its network for three minutes after a restart, it falls back to the setup network.
The bot doesn't answer Check that the channel is in the allow-list on the Settings page, that the command is on, and that the radio settings match your mesh. The bot rate-limits its replies on purpose.
